# The Growth System — Tiered SaaS Upgrade Offer
 
Applies to clients already on the $39/$49 hosting retainer. This is not the initial free-build offer — it's the next step once the retainer relationship is running. When a client upgrades into any tier below, the standalone hosting fee stops being billed separately — it's absorbed into the tier price.
 
**Important framing rule (per Bruce):** This is a service/tooling offer, not a marketing offer. Nothing here promises leads, calls, local recognition, or search rankings — those depend on the client's own business, not on us. Everything below is described as what the system *does* (a real deliverable), never what it *will get them*.
 
---
 
## Eligibility & Timing
 
- **Default:** offer this once a retainer client has been on hosting for **2+ months**.
- **Exception:** if a client shows genuine interest earlier — asks about reviews, mentions missing calls, brings up wanting more online presence — it's fair to offer as early as **1 month in**. Let his own signal drive the timing, don't force it early without one.
- **Not for new/free-build clients.** They still go through the normal sequence first: free build → retainer → then this, once routine.
- One offer moment, not three separate upsells. He picks a tier (or stays on hosting) — this replaces the old plan of pitching GBP, then Review Agent, then chat widget as three separate asks.
---
 
## Tier 1 — $197/mo — "Foundation"
 
What's included:
- Website hosting + ongoing maintenance (this is what the old $39/$49 fee covered — now folded in)
- Google Business Profile setup and ongoing upkeep (posts, hours, info kept current)
- Basic CRM — every customer and job logged in one dashboard instead of scattered across texts and memory
- Missed-call text-back — anyone who calls and doesn't reach him gets an automatic text so the lead isn't just lost
**What this solves:** the site and business listing stop being "set once and forgotten," and a missed call during a job stops meaning a lost inquiry.
 
## Tier 2 — $297/mo — "Growth"
 
Everything in Tier 1, plus:
- AI Review Agent — automatically requests a review after a job is marked complete, and flags/monitors incoming reviews
- Lead follow-up automation — new inquiries get a text/email sequence instead of relying on him to remember to follow up
- Simple monthly activity summary (review requests sent, missed-call texts sent, new leads logged — activity, not results)
**What this solves:** reviews stop depending on him remembering to ask, and a lead who doesn't answer the first follow-up doesn't just fall through.
 
## Tier 3 — $799/mo — "Full System"
 
Everything in Tier 2, plus:
- AI chat widget on the website — visitors get an instant response instead of a form that sits unanswered
- Full CRM & pipeline view — every call, text, site inquiry, and review tied to one customer record, one dashboard
- Priority turnaround on site changes/requests
**What this solves:** this is the "run it without touching it" version — the full Stone Scaling standardized system in one plan.

## Value framing (for your own reference, not verbatim client copy)
 
This is here so you can explain *why* the price is what it is if he asks, using real market comparisons — not for pasting into a pitch as hype copy:
 
| Component | What it'd cost as a separate tool/service |
|---|---|
| Custom website build + hosting | $1,500–3,000 build, $50–100/mo hosting |
| GBP management | $150–300/mo as a standalone service |
| Review request/reputation tool | $150–300/mo (comparable to Podium, Birdeye) |
| Missed-call text-back | $50–100/mo standalone |
| Lead follow-up automation | $75–150/mo standalone |
| AI chat widget | $100–200/mo standalone |
| Full CRM w/ pipeline | $100–300/mo standalone |
 
Stacked separately, Tier 3's components would run well past $799/mo as individual tools. That's a legitimate cost comparison — use it if he pushes on price, don't lead with it.

## Guarantee (service-based, not outcome-based)
 
Per the "we're not marketing" rule — the guarantee is about the service, never about results:
 
- **No contract, cancel anytime**, on any tier.
- **14-day adjustment window** after upgrading — if something agreed on isn't set up the way it was supposed to be, it gets fixed free and fast.
- **No hidden fees** — the tier price is the whole price. Nothing extra shows up later.
## Scarcity (genuine, not manufactured)
 
Full System (Tier 3) clients take real hands-on setup time. State an honest cap on how many can be onboarded in a given month rather than implying artificial urgency — e.g. "only taking on a couple of Full System builds a month right now so each one gets set up right."

*"hey, wanted to run something by you since things have been rolling for a bit now. i put together a bigger system that folds in everything - hosting, your business listing, review requests, missed-call text-back, all in one dashboard instead of piecing it together. runs $197 to $799 a month depending on how much you want handled, no contract either way. want me to break down what's in each?"*
 
Keep it one message, no stacking all three tiers' details unless he asks — let him ask "what's the difference" and answer from there.
