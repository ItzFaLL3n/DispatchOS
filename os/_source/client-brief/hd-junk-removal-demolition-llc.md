## The approach being followed (context for continuity)

This client is being run on a deliberate sequence, established and followed consistently throughout:

1. Deliver real value first, genuinely free, no strings, no pricing attached
2. Follow up after delivery with **zero ask attached** — just checking how it's performing. This step is what actually builds the trust; skipping it turns this into a one-time favor instead of a relationship.
3. Only after that, offer the retainer once, low-pressure, framed as "cancel anytime, no contract"
4. Once hosting/retainer feels routine, offer GBP setup (one-time)
5. Once GBP exists, offer the Review Agent (monthly add-on)
6. Never stack multiple offers in one message. Never repeat a pitch he didn't respond to. Never manufacture urgency.

The bigger-picture goal with this account (and the model going forward): become the one person handling Tyler's entire online presence — website, hosting, GBP, reviews — built through this sequence rather than a bundled pitch, so the relationship becomes sticky through consistently delivering, not through lock-in tactics.
