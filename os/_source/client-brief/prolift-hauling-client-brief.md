## Incident notes — sequence break, Sep 2026

Full breakdown of what happened and why, kept here for continuity since this is the reference case for the new guardrails file (`sequence-integrity-guardrails.md`):

1. **Phase 8 (zero-ask check-in) never happened as its own step.** Delivery message included "we'll discuss the next steps tomorrow," which pre-loaded a sales conversation into the delivery moment. When Samuel asked "what's the next steps?" — a question he only had because of that clause — the reply went straight into the domain/hosting pitch.
2. **Delivery → pitch happened in one continuous ~9-hour conversation**, not the days-apart cadence the playbook assumes. Samuel drove the pace by replying fast, but the check-in step should have happened before pricing came up regardless of his pace.
3. **Given the trust note above** (Samuel flagged the original outreach as scam-looking before replying), the speed of free-site → immediate "next thing worth sorting is..." → pricing risks reading as the same pattern that made him suspicious the first time, even though nothing said was actually bait-and-switch.
4. **Two lines edged toward outcome promises:** "gets you showing up when people search stuff like junk removal near me" and "showing up on google when people search is really the end goal." Per the no-outcome-promise rule, these should describe the mechanism (domains vs. subdomains and search indexing) without promising the result.
5. **DNS explanation ("site can go down till it's fixed") landed immediately after the $49 price quote**, which risks reading as manufactured difficulty nudging toward the higher tier, even though the underlying fact is true and the intent wasn't to manipulate.

**No corrective action needed on the account itself right now** — nothing sent was dishonest, and Samuel hasn't reacted negatively. This is purely a pattern to not repeat. Full guardrails and a pre-send checklist are in `sequence-integrity-guardrails.md` — use it before sending anything in Phase 6–10 on any client going forward.
