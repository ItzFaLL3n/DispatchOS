## Rules of the casebook

- One live **pricing** experiment at a time. Total Property Service holds that
  slot until it resolves.
- Every entry resolves to a recorded outcome — including a null result (client
  goes dormant).
- A violation entry links to the client's `overrides.md`, which carries the
  resulting posture for anyone drafting the next message.
