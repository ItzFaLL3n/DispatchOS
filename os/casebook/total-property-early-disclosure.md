# Total Property Service — early price disclosure

**Started:** ~September 2026 (pre-Dispatch OS)
**Client:** Total Property Service (`../clients/total-property-service/`)
**Type:** live experiment — deliberate deviation from the playbook
**Status:** open — do not run a second early-disclosure test until this resolves

## The deviation

Under `../knowledge/intake-playbook.md`, retainer pricing does not exist before
Phase 9 — "not hinted at, not foreshadowed." For this client, pricing
($39 / $49) was disclosed early because they flagged tight finances during
intake.

This was a conscious choice, not a slip. The reasoning: a client worried about
money may disengage mid-build if the "what happens after free" question sits
unanswered. Disclosing early tests whether transparency retains them better than
the standard withhold-until-Phase-9 sequence.

## What is being measured

- **Does the client reach a delivered site?** (They are currently the one most
  at risk on the intake→delivery rate.)
- **Does early disclosure convert to a retainer as well as, or better than, the
  standard sequence?**

## Decision rule

- If early disclosure converts **as well or better** → the playbook changes, and
  early disclosure becomes an option for finance-flagged clients.
- If it converts **worse** → the Phase 9 withhold rule holds, and now it is held
  for a recorded reason rather than a hunch.

Either outcome gets written here. A null result (client goes dormant) still gets
recorded.

## Constraints

- Only one live pricing experiment at a time
  (`../knowledge/acquisition-strategy.md`). This is it. No second test until
  this closes.
- All other rules still bind — no outcome promises, one ask per message, and no
  manufactured urgency about the client's finances.
