# Growth System

The ascension offer. Service/tooling, not marketing — nothing here promises
leads, calls, recognition, or rankings.

**Canonical depth:** `os/knowledge/growth-system.md`.
**Read first:** the client's `os/clients/<slug>/overrides.md`.

## Tiers (one line each)

- **$197 Foundation** — hosting (folded in) + GBP setup & upkeep + basic CRM +
  missed-call text-back.
- **$297 Growth** — everything in Foundation + AI review agent + lead follow-up
  automation + monthly activity summary.
- **$799 Full System** — everything in Growth + AI chat widget + full CRM &
  pipeline + priority turnaround.

When a client upgrades into any tier, the standalone $39/$49 hosting fee stops
being billed — it's absorbed.

## Hot-path checklist (mirrors growth-system.md)

- [ ] Client on the $39/$49 retainer 2+ months, OR 1+ month with a genuine
      organic signal on record?
- [ ] Not a free-build or brand-new retainer client.
- [ ] One offer moment — do not stack all three tiers' detail unless he asks
      "what's the difference".
- [ ] No outcome language anywhere — deliverables only.
- [ ] Guarantee stated as service terms: no contract, cancel anytime, 14-day
      adjustment window, no hidden fees. Never a results guarantee.
- [ ] The market cost-comparison table is used only if he pushes on price,
      never as the opener.
- [ ] Scarcity, if used, is a genuine monthly onboarding cap.

## Never

Pitch this to a free-build or brand-new retainer client. Promise a result.
Lead with the price-comparison anchor. Break it into three separate upsells.
