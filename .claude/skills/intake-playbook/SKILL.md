# Intake Playbook

The Phase 1–10 sequence for a free-website prospect.

**Read first:** the client's `os/clients/<slug>/overrides.md` — the client may
be on a modified sequence (e.g. early price disclosure, a compromised
sequence, a direct-pitch track that skips this entirely).
**Canonical depth:** `os/knowledge/intake-playbook.md` (phase-by-phase scripts,
samples, gates). Also bound by `os/knowledge/hard-rules.md`.

## The phases (one line each)

1 confirm & kick off · 2 services + contact · 3 photos & socials · 4 site
preferences · 5 domain check · 6 mid-build check-in (no ask) · 7 delivery
(delivery only) · 8 zero-ask check-in (its own message) · 8.5 the bridge (wait
for a client-originated door) · 9 retainer offer (one ask, two options) ·
9b domain trigger · 10 growth system upgrade.

## Hot-path checklist (mirrors intake-playbook.md)

- [ ] `overrides.md` read — is this client on a modified sequence?
- [ ] Which phase is the conversation in? State it explicitly.
- [ ] Is the drafted message allowed at this phase?
      - No pricing / domain / hosting / "next steps" before Phase 9.
      - No ask attached in Phase 6 or Phase 7.
      - Phase 7 delivery message contains delivery and nothing else.
      - Phase 8 zero-ask check-in is its own message — not a clause in
        delivery, not triggered by the client asking "what's next?".
- [ ] One new ask or idea in this message only.
- [ ] Phase 8.5 gate before any pitch: site delivered & live · check-in sent &
      answered · a few days since delivery · PayPal plan link ready · nothing
      asked of him since delivery.
- [ ] `do_not_pitch_until` in the future? No pitch. No exceptions.
- [ ] Client's fast pace tempting a skipped step? Reply fast, keep the step.

## Never

Fold two phases into one message. Retro-fit a zero-ask check-in after a pitch.
Re-pitch a deferral. Explain a technical downside so it reads as price pressure.
